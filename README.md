Entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun) competition
**The Tired Dark Lord: A 100-Word Villain Monologue**
(`u-kevin-the-tired-dark-lord-a-100-word-villain-58b4f3cb`).

A fantasy villain who is secretly just very tired delivers his monologue:
three hundred years holding the Black Spire up, and what really holds it
is mortar and a ledger. The tiredness lives in the rhythm, the pauses and
the small deflections — a wobbling stool, a leaking roof, a cult that meets
on Tuesdays, a drip over his bed — never in a plain statement that he is
weary. He still has a scheme, a history, and a sword waiting for him.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the 102-word monologue (482 characters) to
`/output/entry.txt`, then validates it against the competition gate
before writing: 1–600 characters, non-whitespace, exact expected length
and word count, no leading/trailing whitespace, no double spaces.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

Requires Node 22.18 or later (the grader runs Node 22.23.4).
